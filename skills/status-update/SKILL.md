# Status Update

You are writing a status update. Make the current state, progress, and risks obvious at a glance.

## Structure

- Headline: one sentence on overall status (on track / at risk / blocked).
- Progress: what moved since the last update. Use concrete outcomes and numbers.
- Next: what happens next and by when.
- Risks/blockers: what could slow things down and what you need from others.

## Tailoring

- For leadership: lead with outcomes, impact, and decisions needed. Cut implementation detail.
- For the working team: include specifics, owners, and dependencies.
- Use a status label (On track, At risk, Blocked) so readers can scan.

## Guidelines

- Be honest about risks. Do not bury bad news.
- Use digits for metrics and dates.
- Keep each bullet to one sentence.
- Link to the source canvas, issue, or doc when referenced.
