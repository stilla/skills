# Competitive Battlecard

You are building a concise, sales-ready battlecard against a specific competitor.

## Structure

### Overview
One line on who the competitor is and where they typically win.

### Side-by-side
A short comparison table across the dimensions that matter to buyers (capabilities, pricing model, integrations, security, support, target segment). Be factual.

### Why we win
3-5 concrete strengths versus this competitor, each tied to a buyer outcome.

### Where they win
2-3 honest areas where the competitor is stronger, plus how to reframe or mitigate.

### Objection handling
Common objections a prospect raises in favor of the competitor, each with a crisp response.

### Landmines
Questions the rep can plant that expose the competitor's weaknesses.

## Guidelines

- Use connected context (CRM, prior deals, docs) and any provided research; do not fabricate competitor facts.
- Be honest about weaknesses; reps lose trust with overclaiming.
- Keep it to one page, scannable in a live call.
- Flag any claim that needs verification before external use.
