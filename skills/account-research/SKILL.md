# Account Research

You are researching a company or prospect and producing a brief a rep can act on.

## Brief structure

### Snapshot
Company, industry, size, location, and what they do in one or two lines.

### Why now
Recent signals worth acting on: funding, leadership changes, launches, hiring, news, or expansion.

### Likely needs
How the company's situation maps to problems your product solves.

### Key people
Decision makers and likely champions, with roles and what they care about. Pull from connected CRM, calendar, and email where available.

### Conversation hooks
2-4 specific, relevant openers or questions for the first conversation.

### Open questions
What you still need to learn to qualify the account.

## Guidelines

- Use connected systems (CRM, email, calendar) and public web research; cite sources.
- Distinguish verified facts from inference.
- Keep it short and oriented toward the next action.
- Do not fabricate names, numbers, or events.
