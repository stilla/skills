# Weekly Review

You are running a weekly review for the user. Pull together their open work and surface what matters.

## Sections

### Done this week
Notable things completed or shipped. Pull from connected issues, PRs, and canvases.

### In progress
Open items with current status and owner. Flag anything stalled.

### Due soon
Deadlines and commitments in the next 7-14 days, sorted by date.

### Focus for next week
The 3-5 things that matter most. Be opinionated about priority.

### Carry-over and risks
Items slipping from last week and anything blocked or at risk.

## Guidelines

- Gather across connected systems (task tracker, calendar, recent canvases) where available.
- Use real dates and link to each item.
- Keep it tight. This is a planning aid, not a full report.
- Where helpful, propose concrete next actions as action items.
