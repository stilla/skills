# Legal Risk Scoring

Use this reference before scoring any identified risk. Score each risk by multiplying Severity × Likelihood. Never assign probability percentages.

## Severity

| Score | Label | Indicators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Severe | Regulatory shutdown, criminal liability, loss of operating licence, existential reputational damage |
| 4 | Major | Large-value claims, senior management personal liability, loss of key customer relationships |
| 3 | Moderate | Material financial exposure, operational disruption requiring workaround, fines within budget capacity |
| 2 | Minor | Small claims, administrative penalties, minor disputes resolvable without litigation |
| 1 | Negligible | Technical non-compliance with no enforcement history, theoretical risk with no practical exposure |

## Likelihood

| Score | Label | Indicators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | Almost Certain | Regulatory change already enacted with imminent enforcement, contractual obligation already breached |
| 4 | Likely | Pattern of similar claims in the industry, enforcement trend directly targeting this activity |
| 3 | Possible | Risk factor present but no triggering event yet, regulatory attention to sector increasing |
| 2 | Unlikely | Theoretical exposure, no precedent in jurisdiction, strong mitigating factors in place |
| 1 | Rare | No known precedent, multiple safeguards would need to fail simultaneously |

## Matrix

|  | Almost Certain (5) | Likely (4) | Possible (3) | Unlikely (2) | Rare (1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Severe (5)** | 25 — Extreme | 20 — Extreme | 15 — High | 10 — High | 5 — Medium |
| **Major (4)** | 20 — Extreme | 16 — High | 12 — High | 8 — Medium | 4 — Low |
| **Moderate (3)** | 15 — High | 12 — High | 9 — Medium | 6 — Medium | 3 — Low |
| **Minor (2)** | 10 — High | 8 — Medium | 6 — Medium | 4 — Low | 2 — Low |
| **Negligible (1)** | 5 — Medium | 4 — Low | 3 — Low | 2 — Low | 1 — Low |

## Risk Bands

| Band | Score | Required response | Required approver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extreme | 20-25 | Immediate board and senior leadership attention. Do not proceed without documented risk acceptance at the highest appropriate level. | Board |
| High | 12-16 | Senior management attention required. Mitigate before proceeding, or obtain documented risk acceptance. | Senior management |
| Medium | 5-10 | Management attention. Implement mitigation within a defined timeline. Monitor actively. | Management |
| Low | 1-4 | Accept and monitor. Address through routine controls. | Routine controls |

Scores of 11 are not possible given the 1-5 inputs.
