# Legal Risk Categories

Use this catalogue during risk identification. Walk each category against the scoped activity and record only risks that are specific to the facts provided.

| Category | Sub-categories | Example exposures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contractual | Counterparty default, unfavourable terms, ambiguous provisions, missing protections | Uncapped liability, missing IP ownership clauses, auto-renewal traps |
| Regulatory | Non-compliance, changing rules, enforcement, licensing gaps | GDPR violations, AI Act classification, sector-specific licensing |
| Litigation | Third-party claims, employee disputes, IP infringement, product liability | Pending claims, industry litigation trends, pattern of disputes |
| Intellectual Property | Inbound/outbound infringement, trade secret exposure, patent landscape | Freedom-to-operate gaps, open-source licence conflicts |
| Employment | Wrongful termination, discrimination, works council disputes, non-competes | Restructuring risk, worker classification, cross-border employment |
| Data Protection | Breach exposure, transfer risk, consent validity, retention violations | High-volume personal data, special categories, automated decisions |
| Corporate / Governance | Director liability, shareholder disputes, conflicts of interest | Related-party transactions, board composition, disclosure obligations |
| Competition / Antitrust | Cartel risk, dominance, merger control, state aid | Market concentration, vertical agreements, information exchanges |
| Sanctions / Export Controls | Restricted parties, embargoed jurisdictions, controlled technology, dual-use goods | Screening gaps, export licence requirements, deemed exports, restricted end users |
| ESG / Sustainability Disclosure | Green claims, climate reporting, supply chain due diligence, human rights disclosures | Greenwashing claims, CSRD or SEC disclosure gaps, supplier diligence failures |

For each applicable risk, capture:

* Specific risk event
* Legal or contractual source of the risk
* Exposed party or function
* Existing controls, if supplied by the user
* Basis tag: `[From factual context]`, `[From regulatory framework — cite regulation and article]`, or `[AI assessment — verify with counsel]`
